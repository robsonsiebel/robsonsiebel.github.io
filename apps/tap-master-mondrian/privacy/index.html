Privacy Policy for Tap Master Mondrian

Effective date: 2026-10-30

Tap Master Mondrian is an Android game published by MiniChimera Game Studio (“MiniChimera,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy describes the privacy behavior of Tap Master Mondrian version 2.0 (version code 14).

Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay and preference data locally on your device. This includes information such as scores, mission and challenge progress, gameplay counters, sound settings, and review-prompt state.

This local data is used only to provide the game and remember your progress and settings. It is not uploaded to a MiniChimera server. You can remove it by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

Information MiniChimera collects

MiniChimera does not operate user accounts or a developer backend for Tap Master Mondrian. The game does not ask you to provide a name, email address, phone number, precise location, health information, or financial information during gameplay.

The game does not contain advertising and does not use the Android Advertising ID. MiniChimera does not operate its own gameplay analytics or advertising system for this app.

Google Play Billing and service-provider processing

The game includes an optional non-consumable in-app purchase implemented through Google Play Billing and Unity In-App Purchasing.

Google Play processes the purchase and any payment details. Tap Master Mondrian receives the product identifier and purchase or entitlement result needed to complete the transaction. MiniChimera does not receive your payment-card or bank-account details.

The billing and supporting service components included in the app may process technical service data, including device or other identifiers, approximate location, network information, and diagnostic events. The app’s current Google Play Data safety declaration identifies approximate location and device or other IDs as processed ephemerally for service analytics. Tap Master Mondrian does not request Android location permission, and MiniChimera does not use this technical data for advertising, user profiles, or its own gameplay analytics.

Google and Unity process data under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

External links

The game may open external pages, including Google Play pages and an X/Twitter profile. Those services operate independently and apply their own privacy policies when you use them.

Children

Tap Master Mondrian is listed for a broad audience, including children. The game does not require an account and does not ask children to submit personal information during gameplay. Any optional purchase is handled through Google Play rather than by MiniChimera.

Changes to this policy

If Tap Master Mondrian’s privacy behavior changes, this policy will be updated and its effective date will be revised.
